About

Born in ’94, graphic designer, photographer and human being, that’s me, Tobia.

I was born in Italy, and I have lived there for most of my life. In 2020 I moved to Spain, near Malaga, and my photography adventure officially started from there.

I’ve been passionate about photography more or less always. Photos have always attracted me, much more than paintings and other artistic genres.

My favorite photography is street photography. I find it a fantastic medium of communication, and I think that, if done well, street photography is the most complete photographic genre, because it tells the story of a place and its inhabitants in the most sincere and direct possible way.

In addition to photography, I love to sing and listen to music — a lot of music, I mean a lot.

I love to travel and, despite my job as a graphic designer giving me a lot of satisfaction, I am always convinced that it takes away too much time that I could use to travel and photograph the world.

Hope you enjoy the blog. I do my best to keep myself busy.
