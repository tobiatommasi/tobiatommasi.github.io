Looking into the past

June 18, 2026

I was browsing through my external hard drive when I came across some old folders from 2020–2021. I thought to myself, “Hmm, I never published these photos on the blog.”

So, here they are!

Some are decent, some are not, but you can definitely tell that something was going on in my head at the time… or at least, I think so. Haha.

P.S. I know I’ve been posting mostly old stuff lately, and it might seem like I don’t have anything new to share, but I actually have a few projects I’m working on. I’m just not entirely sure what to do with them yet.

Enjoy!

Would you look at that, I even have some black-and-white.

I’m going to end this post with two of the coolest photos I have from back then.

I especially want to talk about the orange one.

I was experimenting and wasn’t happy with the colours at all, so, out of curiosity, I cranked the white balance slider all the way up… and this happened haha. I was really pleased with the result, so I posted it on Reddit.

A few weeks later, I received a message from a guy telling me he loved the photo and had made a watercolour painting of it. Long story short, he was incredibly kind and mailed me the original painting. Now it hangs on my wall, side by side with the original photograph.
