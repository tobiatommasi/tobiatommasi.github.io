Woof woof, wolf wolf

October 17, 2022

Second time in my life that I have the opportunity to see these beautiful creatures, and this time I have a camera.

The pack is everything.

Despite the unfavorable weather, I am happy to have managed to capture some portraits of these fantastic creatures. They are distinguished from other animals by their intelligence and their extremely efficient hierarchical system. I was stupid to discover that the “leader” is not elected only for physical gifts, but often for his own character.

They are curious but extremely cautious animals, fearful of humans due to our violent past against them. I hope that they will soon be able to repopulate their habitats in a healthy way, giving back to them what we have taken from them.

I conclude with a quote: “If you run away from the wolf, you may meet a bear.”
