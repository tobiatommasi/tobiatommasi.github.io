Short times, long exposures

October 30, 2022

Some moments captured with long exposures, photos of various landscapes of southern Spain.

Living on the Costa del Sol, it is obvious the sea is one of the protagonists of my photographs. So I start with two shots that represent the beauty of the sea and the elements that compose it.

Following some more melancholy sea shots. These shots represent the loneliness and the sense of abandonment that can be found on the beaches during the winter period.

Instead, these are two simple shots taken from my terrace during a sunset a few months ago. I was fascinated by the colors, and to give a touch of magic to the photos I decided to take long exposures.

And always from my terrace I add 2 shots of the supermoon called “Buck Moon”. These shots consist of a long exposure for the background plus a short exposure for the moon.

This instead is the port of Malaga at night. A place full of life even at night.

I conclude with some panoramic shots of the “El Torcal de Antequera”, a truly breathtaking natural karst landscape. The photos are all long exposures although it is not very obvious as the day was very calm the clouds moved slowly.
