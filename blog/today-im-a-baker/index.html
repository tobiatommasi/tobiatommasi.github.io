Today, I’m a baker

August 23, 2026

Today, something a little different from usual: bakery. A small collection of photos I took last year of some of my homemade bread creations.

Bread is a great passion of mine. I love making it (always kneading by hand) and I love eating it. I also enjoy making pastries and sweet breads, but I always try to keep things simple, using only a few ingredients. I like minimalism.

The photos were taken at home with some terrible natural light. So I decided to make them a little more artistic by underexposing them and keeping the tones dark and not too saturated, just to bring a little joy to the eye as well.

Enjoy.
