Malaga carnival, 2023

February 20, 2023

A journey through the streets of Malaga during the carnival. An explosion of colors and fun make this evening eagerly awaited by the people of these place.

Let’s start with a general overview of that night. As I said, colors and joy were the protagonists, it’s been a long time since I’ve seen so many people laughing and joking in a carefree way, I missed it a bit.

There were several groups of themedly dressed people entertaining the audience both before and after the parade.

Note of appreciation for these two fantastic costumes that seem to come straight from the age of joy and lightheartedness. I’ve always had a soft spot for vintage styles, I admit it.

As always then, there are moments of slight tension, when someone in the crowd feels bad, the ambulance arrives and you can’t understand what’s happening. Unfortunately there is always the risk that something will happen when so many people are crammed into one place. However, I used the blue light of the ambulance to try some shots.

I conclude with some shots taken here and there of the young people dressed for the occasion. I admit that I didn’t have much choice but to mainly photograph childrens, but only because they were always in the best positions, like a raised platforms or stuff like that.
