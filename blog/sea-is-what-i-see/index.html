Sea is what i see

February 3, 2023

Always me continuing to search happiness, while waiting for the right moment to release the shutter.

Location: Torremuelle, Spain. It hasn’t been long since I started doing landscape photography, and I still don’t feel confident about my shots. I struggle to understand what I have to look for with my eyes, when I have to start shooting, what I have to shoot. Anyway, something decent still came out.

With these shots I tried to create a surreal landscape, the intention was to make the viewer almost seem to see some mountain peaks coming out of the clouds. I wanted to create the illusion of being elsewhere, far from the sea.

This nice subject instead is a cormorant that I spotted while taking some shots. Unfortunately I managed to take a few pictures of him, because as soon as I got close he got worried and ran away. I didn’t want to disturb him, I just wanted to be part of his beauty.

Simple but representative. I tried for several days to find the right light for this shot, and finally, I found it.
