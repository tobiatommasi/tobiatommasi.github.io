Quick stop at the port

November 16, 2025

A very small series of photos I took at the Benalmádena marina.

There isn’t much to say, I liked the atmosphere and I shot what was there.

Bonus photo that has nothing to do with it: In October I spent a few days in my hometown. I didn’t bring my camera because I had no space in my luggage, so no new shots. But a few days later, I remembered an unpublished photo from my very early days, taken many years ago with a terribly worn-out bridge camera. The photo itself isn’t anything special, but it reminds me of “the nice part of home.”
