Dark was the night, cold was the ground

October 18, 2022

A collection of “street photos” taken at night, mainly silhouettes.

Street photography is the reason why I started shooting, it is definitely the photographic genre that I most admire and the more it belongs to me.

I took these two photos in Scotland, Glasgow and Edinburgh to be precise. Fascinated by the silhouettes and warm colors, I could not miss these shots, while walking through the centers of the two cities.

Another way I love to use to create silhouettes is to use illuminated signs as a backdrop. As you can see, the first photo is not particularly good, subject out of focus, burnt pixels of the advertising sign and bad composition. But it is one of my very first shots, taken with the Canon 70D that I no longer have, and therefore has a great emotional value for me.

Another sentimental shot, rough and poorly done, poorly lit and grainy, but that’s okay. Also one of my first shots, taken with an ultra-cheap bridge camera, during the first week of global quarantine due to COVID -19. Taken just outside the door of my house.

More Spanish shots, focusing more on the moments. The first two shots I took in Malaga, while the last shot I took in Seville. Small detail, that beautiful creature in the center in the last photo is my wife, that saint.
