Life on board

October 12, 2022

I’ve never had a boat, but I’ve always thought I was born for the sea. I’ve been on a boat many times with Captain John, and this time I remembered to bring my camera.

The beauty of traveling on a tourist boat is that you always find new faces, new stories and new shots. As always, when I photograph people, I tried to capture candid moments, even if inevitably some posed shots came out.

This couple immediately caught my eye, they had a great connection, and I can’t deny that they were also very photogenic. They have always been quite isolated from the rest of the group, they probably just wanted to enjoy their romantic getaway in peace.

I also tried to take some pictures of the crew, but they were very busy, especially Captain John with his phone, so I was unable to archive great results.

Last but not least, some portraits of this splendid family. Helped by the light of the sunset that was about to arrive and by the naturalness of these women, I was able to capture some beautiful portraits that I definitely consider the best shots of the day.

It was a beautiful day, I can’t wait to get back on the boat.
