Another quick stop at the port

July 26, 2026

Another day, another stroll around Benalmádena Marina.

A lot has changed since the last time I came here. Well, maybe not that much, but enough. I’ve changed jobs, I’ve changed the way I look at my life, and I bought an old new camera, my trusty Nikon P6000. I’m already in love with it, and every photo in this post was taken with this little gem.

But one thing hasn’t changed: the marina is still the same. Some boats come and go, others stay, and the beautiful little boat “Hermes” is still there, as proud and full of life as ever.
