Sir John Stirling Maxwell gardens

October 20, 2022

An unexpected day on our expected trip to Glasgow. A journey into the natural everyday life of the locals.

A small collection of photos that remind me of a pleasant day, spent observing the joy and passion of those who love nature.

The first photo is one of my favorites, it is proof that we are human, and we are programmed to give life. The lady in question was arranging small pots of freshly blossomed seedlings in her garden, and then she placed the pots in a chair just outside her fence, holding a sign inviting visitors to get a pot for free.

The other two photos instead show the behavior of a father with his son. (To be precise, I’m not 100% sure that the people in the second photo are father and son, I didn’t talk to them to avoid ruining the candid moment, but observing them they appeared to me just like a family).

The first photo is a spontaneous portrait of a couple that has remained very close to my heart. Genuine and sweet, they invited us into their garden, showing us the plants they were taking care of, and being Italians, we ended up talking about tomatoes for several minutes.

The other two shots are simply “stolen shots”, I tried to capture moments of everyday life, just like you do in street photography, even if there were no roads here.

A shot made “on the fly” that does not tell much, but I love the composition and colors of the house.
