Spanish mornings

October 29, 2022

A series of photos that represent my sunday walks in Torremolinos, the place where I live.

In these shots I have captured the essence of this beautiful and warm environment, in fact you will see that most of the photos are composed of warm summer colors and silhouettes that represent the intensity of the sun in this part of the world.

These shots are among the first taken with my old canon 70D, in fact you can see that in some photos the dynamic range is low, but in my opinion these very contrasted photos perfectly express the sense of the torrid summer heat.

These are two more “artistic” shots, let’s say, I wanted to create an almost surreal environment by emphasizing a lot the silhouette effect, so as to make the photos almost illustrations.

And, since a dear friend of mine told me just today that, after seeing my instagram profile, she saw that I am a “cat lover”, I am going to post this nice photo of a stray cat silhouette.

I conclude with some shots representing “the people of the beach in their natural environment”.

In the first photo you can see one of the many beach runners and in the background (if I remember correctly) a finder of precious metals with a metal detector, there are more than there are pictures around here.

In the second photo there is clearly a fisherman, but in the background there is a swimmer who almost looks like a fish out of water.

The last photo makes us understand what is the favorite sport of the people who live here, that is to rest, then get up, go to the beach and rest again.
