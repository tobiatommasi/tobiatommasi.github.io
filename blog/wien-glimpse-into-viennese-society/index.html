Wien, a glimpse into viennese society

November 1, 2026

I spent a few days in Vienna to get away from it all, accompanied by my wife and my dear Nikon P6000. I tried to capture the essence of the city, its dynamism and, in some ways, its melancholy.

I’ve decided to divide this post into three sections. The first one, features the few colour shots I took. The second section will feature some of the more interesting black and white photographs. The final section will be dedicated to black and white “snaps”, which I’ve decided to publish more as reminders than as intentional photographs.
